## go-yaml v4 and libyaml

Source: https://github.com/yaml/go-yaml at commit
`1c17b9cee72bf81ae73a8be50fbd7dd3a9985125` (`go.yaml.in/yaml/v4`).

Scope: Rust ports and adaptations of the upstream YAML emitter, scalar
resolver, node composition, and comment scanner/parser behavior in
`crates/yq-core/src/yaml/emitter.rs`, `resolve.rs`, `decode.rs`, `encode.rs`,
and the modified `saphyr/scanner.rs`, `saphyr/parser.rs`,
`saphyr/char_traits.rs`, and `saphyr/input/`. These files were translated
from Go to Rust and adapted to yq.rs's arena, parser events and host-free
engine; they are not unchanged upstream files.

License: Apache-2.0 for go-yaml contributions, together with MIT for portions
ported from libyaml. The upstream ported files carry
`SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0 AND MIT`. The complete go-yaml LICENSE
and NOTICE follow, including the copyright holders for both layers.

The historical go-yaml v3.0.1 LICENSE is also reproduced verbatim to retain
the full MIT permission text for the libyaml-derived portions. Its source is
https://github.com/go-yaml/yaml/blob/v3.0.1/LICENSE. Its historical Go
filenames correspond to `internal/libyaml/` in the v4 source.
