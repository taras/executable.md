# Decision records

Keep small, dated decision records here, one decision per `NNN-slug.md` file.
Each record states its date, context, the accepted decision and links to the
owning document sections. Link the record from the approving issue or pull
request and from the owning specification.

Record maintainer obsolescence declarations and contract-change trade-offs
before changing the contract text. An obsolescence declaration identifies what
the maintainer declared obsolete; a contract-change record explains the choice
and its trade-offs.

Current rules stay in their owning documents. Records explain decisions; they
do not govern behavior.
